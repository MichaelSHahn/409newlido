01The Residence

Architect-designed, thoughtfully renewed.

Builder
Zbranek Custom Homes
Built
2003
Updated
2017 – 2026

Custom built by Zbranek Custom Homes (now Zbranek & Holt), one of Lakeway’s best-known builders, this 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home pairs architect-designed bones with nearly a decade of thoughtful updates. It’s move-in ready, with the space, finishes and location buyers are looking for.

The 3,161 square feet live mostly on one level, with 10- to 12-foot ceilings and 8-foot doors that make every room feel open and bright. Upstairs, a private suite with its own remodeled bath and large walk-in closet works beautifully as a guest retreat, home office or quiet, separate bedroom.

Nearly every part of the home has been remodeled or updated since 2017. The fully renovated kitchen has quartz countertops and refreshed cabinetry, and it opens to living spaces made for gathering. The primary suite, the downstairs bedrooms and even the laundry room have all been updated. New flooring and fresh paint were finished in 2026, and the upgraded insulation, HVAC and iron front door add comfort and curb appeal. A 2025 hybrid water heater and a hot water recirculation pump deliver efficiency and instant hot water throughout the home.

Outside, the roughly third-acre lot has a lush lawn, an iron-fenced yard and a large patio for relaxing, with plenty of room to play, garden or expand the backyard. The oversized 3-car garage has space for vehicles, storage or a workshop.

There’s no HOA, and the home is zoned to Lake Travis ISD. The location is hard to beat: golf is across the street, and trails and the Lakeway Airpark are both under half a mile away. Lake Travis is less than 2 miles away, H-E-B is about 10 minutes, and the shops and dining along RR 620 and the future Lakeway City Center are close by.
